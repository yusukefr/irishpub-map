## What is Split the G?

Split the G is a casual game enjoyed in Irish pubs and elsewhere using the “GUINNESS” lettering on a Guinness glass. After tasting a sip, you set the glass down and see whether the boundary between the beer and its head crosses the first letter “G.”

How the result is judged varies between places and groups. Treat it as a light-hearted conversation starter in the pub—not as an official Guinness competition or a game with one definitive set of rules.

## A simple way to play

1. Order a Guinness and let it settle.
2. Take one comfortable sip at your own pace.
3. Gently return the glass to the table.
4. See where the boundary between the beer and the head crosses the “G.”

Some people aim for the centre of the “G,” some choose a particular line on the letter, and some count only the first sip. Agreeing on your group’s version before you begin keeps the game easy and fun.

## Why split the “G”?

The name comes from using the boundary between the dark beer and its pale head to visually “split” the first “G” in the word “GUINNESS” printed on the glass.

## About Guinness

Guinness is an Irish stout brand whose history was shaped by Arthur Guinness at St. James's Gate Brewery in Dublin. According to Guinness, Arthur Guinness signed a long-term lease for the brewery in 1759. Guinness Draught is known for its dark ruby-red appearance and creamy head.

## Enjoy it safely in an Irish pub

Split the G is not about drinking quickly or drinking more. Do not chug, pressure anyone to drink, or add extra drinks as a penalty for missing the mark. Put your wellbeing and your own pace first, and respect anyone who cannot or chooses not to drink.

The real point is to savour your Guinness, share a conversation, and enjoy the atmosphere of the pub. Let this small game be an invitation to experience Irish pub culture.
